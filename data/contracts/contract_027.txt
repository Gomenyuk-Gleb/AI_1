""Adjusted Revenues" means, for any Fiscal Quarter, the revenues for such Fiscal Quarter, minus the sum of (i) resale revenues other than revenues generated by services provided by UNE-P lines, (ii) carrier access billing revenues and (iii) reciprocal compensation revenues." ""Available Cash" means the sum as at each calculation date of (i) the unused Commitments and (ii) Cash and Cash Equivalents on hand at Borrower and Holding Company." ""Days Sales Outstanding" means for any Fiscal Quarter (i) accounts receivable as of the end of such Fiscal Quarter multiplied by (ii) 365 divided by (iii) the product of (a) 4 multiplied by (b) the Revenues for such Fiscal Quarter.
